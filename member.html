      Official Membership Contract

      Becoming a Peace & Love Proclaimer means committing yourself to peace, unity, respect, and service. Read the agreement, download it, sign it, then send it to the PLP official email.

      Become an Official Member

      Download the official PLP Membership Agreement, complete it, sign it together with your parent or guardian if required, then email the signed copy to the PLP administration.

      Download Contract

      Note: Please attach the completed and signed contract (PDF or clear photo) before sending your email.

      Application Steps

      1. Download the PLP Membership Contract.
      2. Fill in all required information.
      3. Sign the agreement.
      4. Ask your parent/guardian to sign.
      5. Scan or take a clear photo.
      6. Send it to the official PLP email(plpurugwiro5@gmail.com).
